## Why non-commercial

The landslide layer uses the Historical Landslide Catchments dataset of the Geotechnical Engineering
Office, Civil Engineering and Development Department. GEO confirmed that its Open Data Portal Terms of
Use permit this project to use, copy and redistribute the dataset for non-commercial, educational and
research purposes. The published derived data therefore cannot be licensed more openly than that:
everything in this repository that is not code is non-commercial.
